This part lives in `getting-started/part-two.mdx` with `order: 1`. It renders inside the parent
post and has its own address, `/blog/getting-started/part-two`.

## Ordering parts

Parts are sorted by `order`, then by date. The parent has no `order`. Headings are namespaced
per part, so two parts can both have a "Summary" section without colliding in the table of
contents.

## Linking between parts

A link to [the parent](/blog/getting-started) or to [this part](/blog/getting-started/part-two)
from inside the series scrolls the page instead of reloading it.

:::important
Keep each part self-contained: readers can land on it directly from search or the feed.
:::

## Summary

- One folder per series, `index.mdx` as parent
- `order` on the parts
- Tags on every file: the tag pages count them all
